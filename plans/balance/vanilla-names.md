# Vanilla item names

Every item name in the game, taken from its data assets (`CrabChampions-WindowsNoEditor.pak`, build `0x6921D2F7`). A new item's name must not be one of these, or close enough to be confused with one ("Bounty" vs Bounty Hunter). `python tools/check_names.py` checks the idea cards in `plans/` and CrabMods' items against it.
